Frequently asked

How accurate are the palm, tarot, and astrology readings?

Palm Setu blends real astronomical/astrological calculations with AI-assisted interpretation for entertainment and self-reflection. Readings are not scientific predictions and should not replace professional advice.

Why was I charged, or why can't I access a premium feature?

Check Profile > Current Plan in the app to see your active plan. If a purchase didn't unlock a feature, try Profile > Restore Purchases, which re-checks your purchase history with the app store.

How do I change my birth details?

Go to Profile > Birth Details in the app to update your date, time, and place of birth. This updates your astrology charts and readings going forward.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes - Profile > Account Management > Delete Account in the app permanently removes your account and all associated data.

How do I switch the app's language?

Go to Profile > Language in the app to choose from the supported languages.
